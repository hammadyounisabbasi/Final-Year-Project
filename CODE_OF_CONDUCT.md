# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or the disclosure of another person's private information. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `hammadyounisabbasi@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed proportionately. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
